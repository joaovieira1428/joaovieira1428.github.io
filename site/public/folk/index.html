Background

Something to know about me, I was not always a software developer, long before that my passion in music and culture was a pillar in my identity. This passion arrived since my sister first introduced me to the local folkflore group “Os Moleiros da Ribeira”, this was about 20 years ago in 2006, when I was about 8 years old. At this age I was already playing the accordeon for 2 years and already had a bit of a passion for music, being part of the group only enhanced it. Funny enough, I only started playing the instrument in the group about 3 years ago, all this time I was dancing in the group.

It was around that time that I started to be more activaly part of the group (by being a member of the general assembly and leading the practices) and folklore in general, participating in workshops and trainning courses about the various themes like, how do document and perserve our heritage, how best digitizing our culture, alternative ways to get information like in photos or notarial deeds where they had a probate inventories and how to best keep enxiaty in check when performing.

Fast forwarding to now, the culmunation of this workshops, trainning courses and projects lead to now be a part of the Technical Council of Alta Estremadura and the Federação de Folclore Português (FFP) since April this year.
