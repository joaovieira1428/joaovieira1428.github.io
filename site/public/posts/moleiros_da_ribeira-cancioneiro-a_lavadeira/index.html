A Lavadeira

A song about one of the most important jobs that existed, and in some way still exists today, the washerwomen, these were woman that cleaned the family and a lot of the times, if they worked for someone else, their employer's family clothes as well. Sometimes they used the communial wash house, if one was available in the region, but more often than not, they used the rivers or streams.
