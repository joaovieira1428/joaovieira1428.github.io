Os Moleiros

This is the song that gave us our group name, it is also our most famous song. Since our headquarters sit on an old miller house, and at the beginning of a lane of water millers in the region, it was natural to use this name and song as our brand. This song is about loving a miller, be them a woman or a men, it also has a hidden meaning in the dance where the way they coriographed it almost looks like the miller itself when it was crushing the corn.
